TWESE CHW AI: synthetic demonstration data (CSV export)

SYNTHETIC ONLY. No real patients. Generated by data/make_synthetic.py with --patients 60 --months 6 --seed 1 --end-date 2026-10-03.
Same seed gives the same data on every machine. 60 active patients plus 30 former patients (history only, for the trajectory model), 6 villages.
Condition: hypertension and stroke prevention. Blood sugar is stored but not used.
synthetic_truth.csv holds each patient's hidden trajectory and group labels. It exists for evaluation only; the app never reads it.
