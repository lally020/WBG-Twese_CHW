01 / OUR APPROACH

Continuity of care,
one check-in at a time.

Compass supports a patient-facing SMS journey, a prioritized follow-up queue for CHWs, and clinician-supervised escalation. It does not diagnose illness or substitute for emergency care.

02 / TESTING & LEARNING

Show the evidence.
Not just the algorithm.

In the demo, every patient is fictional. The evaluation workspace tracks BP history, self-reported adherence, quiz understanding and held-out classification results. Real clinical outcomes require prospective validation.
